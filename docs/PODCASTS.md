# Podcasts

Podcasts is a local library of shows and optional membership-only entries. It supports name, optional whole-number personal ratings from 1–5, ordered Listening/Stopped status, publishing activity, S/M/L/XL length, release frequency, parent categories and subcategories, membership availability/tier/price, and website. The TV-style toolbar switches between List and Pivots; search shows matching/total entries and `.` focuses it. Compact list rows support inline rating and colored listening-status edits, colored publishing status, one-line membership, a same-line link symbol, publisher and last episode. The table combines user category labels with distinct feed subcategories, and replaces episode/source duration columns with estimated Weekly Time. Cost retains annualized pricing; Web uses a labeled website symbol. Show Status, Last Episode and Weekly Time have two-line headers. The table fills the remaining viewport height and adjusts when banners or filters change. Headers stick inside the vertically scrolling table; all columns resize by dragging their header edge or using arrow keys, with device-local widths retained in full backups and excluded from content sync. Removed table fields remain readable in saved data. Rating/name columns stay visible during horizontal scrolling. Status sorting follows Listening then Stopped. Pivots group matching entries by category, listening/publishing status, length, frequency, membership and publisher; counts include unrated entries, averages exclude them, and weighted scores use the movie pivot formula. Group search, minimum, sorting and group-to-list filtering are device-local. Search includes saved and catalog names, publisher, categories and membership tier. Filters and sort direction are device-local; full backups retain them, content sync excludes them.

## Personal values and catalog metadata

The saved fields and `catalog` snapshot are separate. Linking or reading RSS never overwrites a supplied name, listening status, category, length, frequency, website or membership price. Unknown size/activity/frequency and empty website use catalog values when available; setting Unknown or clearing website returns them to automatic display. The combined Category cell uses personal parent/subcategory labels and appends feed subcategories without case-insensitive duplicates, alphabetizes the merged subcategory list, and bolds subcategories present in the public feed. Table cells use `Category > Subcategory, Subcategory`, separating multiple parent groups with semicolons. Feed subcategories attach to the matching personal parent when available, otherwise the first personal parent; without a personal parent the cell shows `— > Subcategories`; feed parent labels remain available in the editor, and do not replace user categories. Category search, filters and pivots use the combined labels. The editor places catalog identity, public feed facts and refresh actions first, then uses two desktop columns: personal text/prices on the left and all dropdown properties in one aligned vertical stack on the right. Rating buttons set 1–5 in one click; clicking the selected rating clears it. RSS URL/file tools are grouped under Public RSS options. Manual activity is independent of personal listening status.

Categories use one editor line per parent: `Technology > Software, Hardware`. Multiple parents are supported. Flat catalog genres remain parent labels; RSS preserves the published hierarchy. Membership availability is Yes/No/Unknown. A funding URL alone never sets Yes, and a missing price never becomes zero. Price includes a currency and Annual/Monthly/Variable/Unknown billing basis. Annual costs in list rows display as `$` plus the amount with no forced trailing cents or estimate suffix; unknown totals display `—`. Stored currency and monthly annualization are explained in the cell tooltip and editor; no currency conversion occurs. Annual prices display directly, monthly prices display ×12 as an estimate, and variable or unknown billing has no guessed annual total. The optional checked date records manual price verification. The source import's prices are not verified against current offers.

S is under 30 minutes; M 30–under 60; L 60–under 90; XL 90 or more. RSS estimates use up to 20 most recent dated full episodes, excluding explicit trailer/bonus entries, duplicate identities, invalid dates and future episodes. At least three positive valid durations are needed for the arithmetic mean. Four distinct dates are needed for frequency: median gaps through 1.5/4/9/18/40 days map to Daily/Several times a week/Weekly/Every two weeks/Monthly; longer or inconsistent gaps are Irregular. Seasonal remains a manual option. Labels shorten to Multi/Week and 2 Weeks without changing saved values. Weekly Time multiplies catalog mean duration by Daily ×7, Multi/Week ×3, Weekly ×1, 2 Weeks ×0.5 or Monthly ×12/52; the displayed value rounds to the nearest half hour with one decimal (for example, 1.5 hr or 2.0 hr). Frequency precedes Weekly Time; missing durations and Unknown/Seasonal/Irregular frequencies show no estimate. Imported Time retains its unspecified semantics and is never used in that calculation.

A publisher completion flag yields Inactive with an explanation. Otherwise four dated episodes permit a recency estimate: Active within max(90 days, three median gaps), Inactive beyond that window. Sparse history stays Unknown. Recency estimates do not establish permanent cancellation. The list exposes publisher and last episode; the editor also shows catalog categories, inferred length/frequency/activity, website, last episode and sample details. A partial feed retains previously usable observations; failed requests preserve the entire prior snapshot.

## Discovery and public feeds

`core/podcast-catalog.js` uses Podcast Index's keyless Apple-compatible search/lookup endpoints. The numeric `appleId` is an Apple catalog ID returned by Podcast Index, not a Podcast Index feed ID. Feed URL is a separate identity. Requests omit credentials, serialize at least 1.2 seconds apart, time out after 15 seconds and back off for a minute after 429. Search caches are transient. Search results require an explicit title/publisher choice; no runtime title-only auto-linking. Stop, close and query changes cancel requests, and stale responses cannot replace edited drafts.

Live browser search was verified from the local preview. Public endpoints can change or become unavailable. RSS servers independently control CORS; network failures cannot be distinguished reliably from CORS failures by browser JavaScript. Refresh reports both possibilities and keeps saved metadata. Public RSS options include an Open Public Feed link for saving XML and reading it locally. Feed requests bypass the browser HTTP cache, distinguish HTTP errors/rate limits/timeouts, and upgrade HTTP feed URLs to HTTPS when the app runs on HTTPS (including GitHub Pages). This does not bypass publisher CORS restrictions; no third-party proxy is used. Responses/files are limited to 5 MiB. XML DTD/entity declarations are rejected, feed HTML is never rendered, and episode audio is never downloaded. HTTP(S) URLs with embedded credentials or credential query keys are rejected; public RSS URLs only, with restricted query parameters. Unusable feed URLs are omitted from catalog candidates while preserving their safe catalog identity. Private subscription feeds are unsupported.

References: [Podcast Index API](https://podcastindex-org.github.io/docs-api/), [RSS fields](https://help.apple.com/itc/podcasts_connect/en.lproj/itcb54353390.html), [funding tags](https://github.com/Podcastindex-org/podcast-namespace/blob/main/docs/tags/funding.md). Apple-compatible search `releaseDate` is deliberately not used as a show's latest publication date; feed episode dates provide that evidence.

## Supplied-table importer

Podcasts → Import Podcasts accepts the supplied 11-column Markdown or tab-separated table, pasted or loaded from a file. It also accepts a prepared JSON envelope: `{format: "top-shelf-podcasts-import", version: 1, podcasts: [...], audit: [...]}`. This is a podcast-table importer, not an arbitrary spreadsheet or full-app restore.

- Active TRUE/FALSE maps to Listening/Stopped, as confirmed by the user. It never sets publishing activity.
- Len. preserves S/M/L/XL. Couple maps to Several times a week, Every Other to Every two weeks, Random to Irregular. Original recurrence text, Last, Time, source name and source URL remain in `legacy`; Last and Time have no invented semantics.
- Costs are the supplied annual USD amounts. Blank/— values mean unknown. A named membership or supplied price records availability; membership-only rows with membership/plus URLs remain separate entries.
- Blank/footer/totals rows are ignored with audit messages. Invalid columns, values, URLs, duplicate row IDs and oversized inputs fail before application. Markdown bold, `<br>`, nonbreaking-space entities and Markdown links are handled without rendering HTML.
- IDs/source keys derive from the source name for repeatable imports. Preview shows values, candidate matches, selected additions/links and explicit skip reasons. Matching can be searched per row with an editable query or for selected unlinked shows, and stopped. Existing catalog IDs/feed URLs cannot be assigned twice.
- Reimporting an existing unlinked source entry attaches only catalog metadata. Existing personal edits, linked rows and tombstones are kept. Reused IDs for unrelated names and same-name entries without matching source identity are skipped for review rather than silently merged.
- Application saves recovery, checks the preview and browser storage for intervening edits, validates the final library and persists before reporting success. Cancelling/closing during recovery prevents application. Failed writes retain live data and the editable preview.

Personal source, candidate results, prepared JSON and feed downloads belong in ignored `import-preparation/`. They are never bundled, seeded automatically, or committed as runtime assets. Importing and optional GitHub Sync remain separate actions.

## Persistence and verification

Local schema 10 adds `workspace.podcasts`; supported older local schemas migrate with an empty podcast library. Local schema 11 adds optional personal ratings without altering existing entries. Cloud v11/schema 15 protects podcast ratings from older clients silently dropping them; v10/schema 14 introduced podcasts. All v1–v10 cloud formats remain readable, including v9 formatted Notes. The storage key stays compatible. Full backup/restore, content hashing, merge, import counts, preference reset and recovery include podcasts. Conflicting records or duplicate linked identities require a choice; timestamps do not choose winners. Delete saves recovery before creating a tombstone. Editor and import guards reject concurrent changes.

Run `node scripts/check.mjs`. With Playwright available, serve the preview and run `node tests/podcasts.browser.cjs` (optional `PODCAST_TEST_URL`). The isolated browser suite uses synthetic data and mocked providers, checking list/pivots, search shortcut and counts, inline rating/status, compact rows, RSS enrichment, import/link, repeat imports, stale drafts, failed writes, backup/content round trips, desktop/390/320px layout, offline edit/reload and recovery deletion. No runtime dependency is added. Real multi-device/authenticated GitHub Sync requires separate verification.

The toolbar has Add and Refresh buttons; Import Podcasts is removed from the toolbar. The Refresh button refreshes linked public RSS feeds and shows the most recent successful feed date in parentheses on the same line (Never updated before any success). Refresh keeps personal fields and prior snapshots for failed feeds, saves recovery before applying successful results, and rejects concurrent saved-data changes. Stop cancels the batch without applying pending results. A partial success reports refreshed/failed counts and the first affected show with its failure reason; a failed refresh never advances the timestamp.

Podcast alphabetical list and pivot sorting ignore a leading “The” (case-insensitive); displayed and saved names stay intact. Name ties use the same rule. During bulk refresh, each linked row turns green after success, red after failure with no prior successful feed read, or yellow after failure when a previous successful feed snapshot exists. Icons include a status label/tooltip. Completed results are device-local preferences retained in full backups and excluded from content sync, scoped to the linked feed URL. Cancelled/stale/failed-save batches discard their provisional colors. Previously fetched feeds show green when no newer local result exists.

My Status shows the count of Listening entries in the filtered table. Weekly Time shows the sum of the displayed half-hour estimates for all filtered rows, including Stopped entries. Unknown estimates are excluded and counted in the total tooltip; a nonempty set with no known estimates shows `—`, while an empty table totals `0.0 hr`. Both header totals update with filters, search, inline edits and refreshes.

The Name field has a Search button that sends the typed name to catalog lookup and presents matches for explicit selection. Successful feed refresh immediately displays inferred Show Status, Length, Frequency and Website in automatic property controls while their stored personal fields remain Unknown/blank; saving does not turn those observations into manual overrides. Explicit control changes become manual values; choosing Unknown returns to automatic display. Existing manual choices, prices, personal status and categories are preserved. Failed/stale/stopped refreshes do not update controls.
